How to measure guide

 

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Take your actual body measurements as they are more accurate than measuring over your clothes.
  2. There may be +/- 0.5 inches/cm variations possible depending on the garment fabric type
  3. If you're on the borderline between two sizes, order the smaller size for a tighter fit or the larger size for a looser fit

Hold the tape horizontally to measure

Hold the tape vertically to measure

  1. NECK:

    Measure at the base of your neck where the garment collar would sit.

  2. SHOULDER:

    Measure from one tip of the shoulder to other.

  3. CHEST:

    Measure under your arms, around the fullest part of your chest

  4. WAIST:

    Measure around the narrowest part of your waistline, above navel below ribcage.

  5. HIP:

    Measure around the fullest part of your hips, keeping the feet close together.

  6. SLEEVE LENGTH:

    Measure from the tip of your shoulder to your wrist

  7. LENGTH:

    Measure from the base of the collar, till hem of the garment.
